Reading a page aloud

Edge's Read Aloud reads a page to you, highlighting each word as it is spoken, and keeps the sentence being read on screen. This page tries the same in any webview, with the voices the system already has.

Ask Dr. Moore, e.g. by mail, whether the Premium voices are worth the download. They are free, and they sound far better than the voices a Mac starts with.

What gets read

  • Paragraphs, headings, and list items, each on its own.
  • Text inside links, emphasis and inline code, read straight through.
  • Table cells, one after another.

What does not

Anything hidden (like this text, which must never be read), anything marked decorative and with aria-hidden, and anything an app opts out.

Scrolling along

This box scrolls on its own, inside the page. While it is read, the sentence being read is kept in view within the box.

Scroll by hand while reading, and the reader leaves the page where you put it for a few seconds before following along again.

Changing the speed or the voice starts again from the word being spoken, so nothing is missed and nothing is repeated twice.

Pausing holds the place. Skipping moves a whole sentence at a time, forward or back.

That is the end of the box.

And that is the end of the page.
